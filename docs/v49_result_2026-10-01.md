# V49: V17 pipeline unreconstructable; cross-architecture ensemble still ~0.37; hold 0.39

Recorded 2026-10-01 IST. Continued research toward beating V17 (tune PQ 0.448703
-> public 0.39) after V46-V48 negatives.

## V17 reconstruction feasibility (NEGATIVE)
kaggle_inference/v17_compose.py (run_compose) IS on the VM, but it requires a
SHA-256-pinned input set that is NOT present: V6 yolo_best.pt, two V16 arm_r1_best.pt
detectors, the V11 arm_e4.pt refiner, and 321 frozen raw proposal caches. A VM-wide
search found NONE of these (they were /kaggle/input artifacts, wiped). Therefore
V17's exact 0.4487 six-source pipeline CANNOT be reproduced on this VM. The V21
checkpoints present are the full-REFIT variants (different hashes; V21 scored public
0.38, below V17).

## Cross-architecture ensemble (NEGATIVE)
The V21 V17-recipe detector IS a loadable seg model. Standalone tune PQ 0.351
(recall 0.559 -- higher recall than V44's 0.48). Ensembled with V44 (yolo11s-seg,
a different architecture) via agreement filtering: best PQ 0.370 (conf0.2/agree2/
hc0.7), gain +0.010 over best single, still ~0.08 below V17. Even cross-ARCHITECTURE
models hit the same ~0.37 ceiling: complementary recall (up to 0.59) is always
cancelled by correlated/added false positives.

## Research (NEGATIVE for the hard bucket)
arXiv 2412.04243 shows SAM/foundation models fail precisely on "tree-like, low
textural contrast" objects == solar filaments, so SAM2 prompting would not recover
the invisible-18.7% hard-FN bucket. EdgeAttNet preprocessing (V48) gave 0 recall lift.

## Conclusion
Six independent attempts (V44 single, V45 same-family union, V47 inference gating,
V48 preprocessing, precision agreement, V49 cross-arch) all cap at ~0.35-0.37 tune
PQ. V17's 0.4487 is a property of its specific SIX-source cache ensemble, which is
not reconstructable here. Nothing beats public 0.39 -> DO NOT SUBMIT. V17 submission
56103401 preserved at 0.39. Evidence: outputs/v49_crossarch/, outputs/v46_research/.
