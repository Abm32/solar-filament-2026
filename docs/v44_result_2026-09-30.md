# V44 result — full-population train does NOT generalize; keep V17 0.39

Recorded 2026-09-30 IST. Trained the V42 recipe (yolo11s-seg @ 2048) on ALL 566
canonical fit files (one max-instance record per physical image, 4,452 instances),
200 epochs, then evaluated on the held-out 141-file canonical TUNE split with the
project PQ metric (disjoint non-overlapping instance decode, IoU>0.5 matching).

## Held-out tune trajectory
| epoch | tune PQ | recall | medIoU | best conf |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 31  | 0.3506 | 0.493 | 0.650 | 0.40 |
| 65  | 0.3568 | 0.491 | 0.658 | 0.30 |
| 102 | 0.3485 | 0.457 | 0.661 | 0.50 |
| 200 | 0.3510 | 0.480 | 0.654 | 0.20 |

Even at confidence 0.05 the epoch-65 recall was only 0.528 with 494 FP: a genuine
detection ceiling on held-out data, not a confidence or undertraining artifact.

## Verdict
beats_v17 = FALSE. V17 tune PQ is 0.448703 (-> public 0.39); V44 plateaus at
~0.35 tune PQ, roughly -0.09 BELOW V17. The spectacular V42/V43 FIT numbers
(recall 0.997, PQ 0.768 on 16 images) were memorization and did NOT generalize:
the frozen fit gate (PQ>=0.85) that V42/V43 FAILED correctly predicted this
non-generalization. A single yolo11s-seg model is weaker than V17's six-source
proposal ensemble.

## Decision
Do NOT submit V44 — it would regress below public 0.39. Preserve V17 submission
56103401 at 0.39. Recall wall on held-out data remains the true bottleneck; any
future gain needs a mechanism that generalizes (ensemble/new foreground), not a
single-model overfit. Evidence: outputs/v44_full_population/.
