# V25 research — suppress false background gradients without adding positives

## Why V24 did not close the missing-label hypothesis

V24 changed two things simultaneously from the learner's perspective: it removed false background supervision and promoted every cross-rater singleton to a positive. It moved raw recall and TP in the predicted direction, confirming signal in the disputed regions, but FP increased more than PQ could tolerate. The negative result closes consensus-positive training, not uncertainty-aware loss weighting.

## Primary literature synthesis

Soft Sampling (Wu et al., 2019) shows that missing objects depress detector confidence because their proposals are trained as background. It preserves true background while reducing uncertain gradients, outperforming hard-negative deletion and improving Open Images. Background Recalibration Loss (Zhang et al., 2020) directly studies one-stage dense detectors and finds them more vulnerable than two-stage methods because every anchor contributes. Its strongest ablation uses a 0.1 confusing-anchor scale; zero and 0.01 underperform, establishing that complete removal is too aggressive.

The Solar dataset offers stronger uncertainty localization than generic missing-label datasets: another expert explicitly labeled the region in the identical image. Therefore V25 need not infer confusing anchors from model confidence or broad IoU heuristics. It can downweight only negative anchor centers inside known omitted-rater medoid boxes.

## Why not full BRL gradient reversal

BRL mirrors the negative focal branch below a score threshold, effectively trusting model confidence as weak positive evidence. V24 already showed that increasing disputed-region positivity creates excessive FP in this task. V25 first tests the conservative intervention: 0.1 negative weight with no positive gradient. This cleanly separates suppression of incorrect negatives from positive harvesting.

## Risks priced by the protocol

- Single-rater annotations may be false positives: they never become V25 positives.
- Bounding boxes include background around thin masks: weight is 0.1, not zero, and only classification is affected.
- Augmentation could misalign metadata: sentinels are inserted as ordinary instances before transforms and split afterward.
- Custom-code differences could confound results: matched control uses identical custom runtime with weight 1.0 at both seeds.
- Recall may rise with FP: raw-recall, matched-PQ, absolute-PQ, and replication gates must all pass before audit.
