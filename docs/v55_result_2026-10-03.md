# V55 — precision-focused V52 retrain (Tversky FP-penalty + focal + clDice): NEGATIVE

Date: 2026-10-03. VM: rogii-gpu (4× T4). Firewall intact: FIT-only training; no tune
labels in training; no test / CSV / submission; rogii not deallocated.

## Hypothesis
V54 showed V52's false positives cannot be filtered post-hoc (a FIT-fit quality gate
≈ raw mean-prob, AUC 0.822). The remaining lever: change WHAT THE MODEL IS CONFIDENT
ABOUT by retraining with a precision-focused loss, cutting V52's FP (base32 519, w1 364)
at the source so the V53 fusion recall path (beta>0) could finally go net-positive.

Loss (scripts/train_v55_precision_semantic.py, imports V52 machinery, V52 untouched):
`focal_bce(gamma) + tversky(alpha_fp, beta_fn) + cldice_w*soft_clDice`, with
alpha_fp > beta_fn to penalise FP pixels harder than FN (precision bias). Checkpoints
V52-compatible -> evaluated via V52 `--mode tune`.

4 variants trained FIT-only, 140 epochs, res 1024, base 32 (all 4 T4s):
tv73 (α0.7/β0.3, cl1.0, γ2) · tv82 (α0.8/β0.2, cl1.0, γ2) ·
tv64_cl2 (α0.6/β0.4, cl2.0, γ2) · tv82_cl2 (α0.8/β0.2, cl2.0, γ2).

## Result: precision training BACKFIRED on instance FP
Standalone tune PQ (141 held-out files, V52 tune mode, prob sweep):

| model | best thr | PQ | recall | TP | FP | FN |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| V52 base32 | 0.40 | 0.3687 | 0.624 | 715 | 519 | 430 |
| **V52 w1 (baseline, best source)** | 0.60 | **0.3766** | 0.562 | 643 | **364** | 502 |
| V55 tv73 (α0.7) | 0.30 | 0.3709 | 0.595 | 681 | 632 | 464 |
| V55 tv82 (α0.8) | 0.30 | 0.3609 | 0.575 | 658 | 648 | 487 |
| V55 tv64_cl2 (clDice-w2.0) | 0.70 | 0.0056 | 0.011 | — | 1407 | — |

- Both converged variants (tv73, tv82) have **HIGHER FP** (632/648) than both V52
  baselines (519/364) at **LOWER recall** — the exact opposite of the design goal.
- Higher α (tv82, 0.8) was worse than moderate α (tv73, 0.7) on every axis.
- clDice-w2.0 variants collapsed: tv64_cl2 tune PQ **0.0056** (near-dead); its training
  Tversky oscillated 0.99→0.70→0.99 (unstable), never learning region overlap. tv82_cl2
  equally poor (training Tversky ~0.87). Both discarded.

## Mechanism (the real learning)
A pixel-level Tversky FP-penalty does **not** reduce instance-count FP after the semantic
pipeline's threshold → connected-components step. Penalising FP *pixels* makes the model
predict **smaller, fragmented** masks: that lowers recall AND creates MORE small
boundary-fragment components, each counted as a separate FP instance. V52's instance FPs
are confident, filament-shaped hallucinations in low-contrast regions — a *confidence/
localisation* error that a pixel-reweighting loss cannot remove. **Pixel-precision ≠
instance-precision** for a semantic→connected-components PQ pipeline.

This both (a) confirms the mid-training learnings prediction that clDice-w2.0 would fail
(docs/v55_learnings_2026-10-03.md §2a, §3.1 — now with PQ 0.0056 as the strongest
possible confirmation) and (b) adds a new finding: the precision-Tversky direction itself
is counterproductive for *instance* PQ on this pipeline, independent of the clDice weight.

## Decision
Do **NOT** run the V55 → V17 fusion grid. Per the learnings-doc rule (don't fuse a source
that fails to beat V52's FP at matched recall), every V55 variant is strictly worse than
V52 w1 as a source. Fusion would at best reproduce V53's +0.0006 with a worse input —
negative expected value. V55 is closed as a negative. Keep V17 `56103401` at public 0.39.

Evidence: `outputs/v55_*/tune.json`, `outputs/v55_*/train.json`,
`docs/v55_learnings_2026-10-03.md`.

## What remains (lower EV, future sessions)
The three post-V17 orthogonal-source attempts now bound the problem precisely:
- V53: V52 IS orthogonal to V17 (FP-corr 19–24%, recovers 93/317 FNs) but mean-prob can't
  gate its FPs → fusion +0.0006.
- V54: no cheap post-hoc score separates V52's TP/FP better than mean-prob.
- V55: precision-focused *training* raises instance-FP (pixel≠instance precision).

Levers not yet tried, in rough EV order:
1. **Instance-aware training**, not pixel-aware: a detection/instance head on the semantic
   features (or a watershed/offset-regression head) so the FP penalty acts on *instances*.
   This is the direct fix for the pixel≠instance mechanism — but it's a new architecture,
   not a loss tweak.
2. **Confidence calibration via deep ensembles / MC-dropout** on V52 to down-weight the
   low-contrast hallucinations (attacks the confidence error directly). Moderate cost.
3. **Skeleton-Recall loss** (noise-robust; MAGFiLO ~18.7% noisy labels) replacing clDice,
   at clDice-w≤0.5 with warm-start Dice→Tversky(β>α) and γ=4/3 (docs §4). Cheapest, but
   given V55's backfire its EV for *fusion* is now low.
