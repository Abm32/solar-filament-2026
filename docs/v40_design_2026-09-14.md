# V40 design — multiscale dark-ridge signal opportunity gate

Frozen **2026-09-14 before reading tune image pixels or implementing V40**.

## Decision and separation from closed work

V39 showed that rearranging existing detector evidence cannot break the barrier:
113/212 all-source misses have zero admissible proposal fragment, and perfect graph
recovery projects V17 tune PQ only to 0.464294. A new model must generate foreground
from image pixels.

V40 is a CPU-only opportunity audit for a genuinely new native-resolution semantic
foreground generator. It asks whether detector-unsupported GT instances contain a
repeatable dark curvilinear image signal under a fixed multiscale ridge transform.
It does not train a semantic model, reopen V23, or authorize submission by itself.

V23 tested the resource feasibility of retraining YOLO on radial-flattened/CLAHE
images and stopped before quality evaluation because every 896/batch-1 T4 arm
exceeded its strict memory gate. Therefore V23 did not measure whether image-level
ridge evidence exists in the 212 misses. V40 uses V23's already frozen, label-blind
competition-only transform only as normalization before a new ridge measurement.

- public full-MAGFiLO labels/images, solar checkpoints, and public payloads;
- GPU/model training, CSV generation, export, and submission.

The report must record `reads_tune_images=true`, `reads_fit_images=false`,
`reads_audit=false`, `reads_test=false`, `trains=false`, `exports=false`, and
`submits=false`.

## Frozen image transform

For each 2048x2048 grayscale tune JPEG, apply V23's frozen label-blind transform:
solar-disk localization, 256-bin radial-median background flattening, p2-p98 robust
scaling, Gaussian sigma 0.7, CLAHE clip 2.0 / grid 8x8, and off-disk zeroing. V40
must import this implementation unchanged and record its constants.

## Frozen multiscale ridge response

For each GT instance independently:

1. Rasterize the canonical GT mask and project/densify its expert spine using V31's
   frozen radius-3 method.
2. Crop the transformed image to the GT bounding box plus **48 px** margin. This
   exceeds the largest ridge scale and the local-control ring.
3. Compute scikit-image Frangi vesselness on the crop at native resolution with
   `sigmas=(1,2,4,8)`, `alpha=0.5`, `beta=0.5`, `gamma=None`, and
   `black_ridges=true`. No scale or parameter sweep is permitted.
4. Define the positive region as a disk-radius-2 dilation of the projected expert
   spine, intersected with the GT mask.
5. Define local clean background as pixels whose Euclidean distance outside this
   GT is in **[8,24] px**, excluding every official annotation mask from every
   annotator record for the same physical JPEG. This reduces contamination from
   neighboring labeled filaments.
6. Require at least 10 positive pixels and 100 clean-background pixels.

The response is addressable when all hold:

- local ROC AUC (positive ridge pixels versus clean background) >= **0.70**;
- at least **25%** of positive pixels exceed the clean-background 90th percentile;
- median positive response is strictly above median clean-background response.

AUC uses deterministic average ranks. Equality contributes 0.5 through rank
averaging. These thresholds are frozen as a moderate but nontrivial signal:
random/uninformative response has AUC 0.5 and only 10% expected q90 exceedance.

## G0 — integrity and regression

Require:

- canonical annotation SHA-256 and exact 94/150/1,134 tune population;
- V39 bytes match the pinned SHA-256;
- V39 groups reproduce 922 found / 212 missed and exactly 113 misses with zero
  admissible fragments;
- exactly 94 distinct tune JPEGs are opened, all 2048x2048 grayscale;
- all projected spines are valid and all local regions meet sample-count minima;
- synthetic dark-line response exceeds bright-line and flat controls;
- synthetic AUC/rank and crop-order invariance checks pass;
- every value is finite and repeated execution is deterministic.

Any failure invalidates V40.

## G1 — material image-signal opportunity gate

G1 passes only if all hold:

1. at least **65%** of all 212 all-source misses are ridge-addressable;
2. at least **65%** of the 113 zero-admissible-fragment misses are
   ridge-addressable—the mechanism must add genuinely new evidence;
3. at least **80%** of the 922 found-by-any instances are ridge-addressable,
   establishing that the fixed signal recognizes known filament structure;
4. perfect zero-FP recovery of every addressable all-source miss at IoU **0.65**
   projects V17 tune PQ >= **0.50**;
5. among addressable zero-fragment misses, median AUC >= **0.75** and median q90
   exceedance coverage >= **0.35**;
6. G0, finite-output, determinism, and firewall checks pass.

V17 arithmetic remains frozen at IoU sum 481.00931292355114, 704 TP, 306 FP, and
430 FN. The projection is an opportunity ceiling, not expected model performance.

## Conditional G2

Only passing G1 authorizes a separately frozen bounded 16-record overfit design for
a new native-tile semantic generator. That design must specify architecture,
generic-pretraining provenance, ridge/raw input channels, foreground and
connectivity losses, semantic-to-instance reconstruction, memory/runtime caps,
exact train PQ/recall/FP gates, and no tune/audit/test access. V40 G1 itself never
trains a model.

## Stop rules

- Do not alter the V23 normalization, Frangi scales/parameters, 48 px crop, spine
  radius, control ring, AUC 0.70, q90 coverage 0.25, population gates, recovery
  IoU, or projected-PQ gate after observing results.
- Do not inspect images manually to relabel failures or choose scales.
- Do not use public MAGFiLO data or solar-specific weights.
- Do not read audit/test, train, export, or submit if G1 fails.
- If G1 fails, close this fixed classical ridge signal. Any further image model
  must have a separately researched learned feature mechanism, not a threshold
  rescue of V40.

Primary method source: Frangi et al., *Multiscale vessel enhancement filtering*,
MICCAI 1998. Solar preprocessing context: Solomon et al., *EdgeAttNet: Towards
Barb-Aware Filament Segmentation*, 2025.

## Final result — closed at G1

The canonical tune audit opened exactly 94 official competition train JPEGs and
processed all 150 annotator records / 1,134 instances. V39 population regression
reproduced 922 found, 212 missed, and 113 zero-fragment misses. All projected
spines and local clean-background regions were valid.

The fixed Frangi signal was ridge-addressable for **226/922 (24.5119%)** known
found instances, **63/212 (29.7170%)** all-source misses, and **40/113 (35.3982%)**
zero-fragment misses. Addressable zero-fragment cases had strong median AUC
**0.811453** and q90 coverage **0.408248**, but the mechanism was not broad:
found-control, miss-coverage, and zero-fragment-coverage gates all failed. Perfect
zero-FP recovery of all 63 addressable misses at IoU 0.65 projects V17 tune PQ
only **0.448703 -> 0.473003**, below 0.50.

V39 graph and V40 ridge opportunities overlap on 12 misses and jointly cover
91/212, leaving 121 unsupported. Among the 113 zero-fragment misses, 73 also lack
V40 ridge addressability.

Therefore `g1_passed=false` and `g2_model_design_authorized=false`. Close this
fixed multiscale classical ridge path without model training. Repeated execution
was byte-identical; result SHA-256 is
`5362ac80b483de0e45c32a82177d4a9161b1526848635488b6429585f222092b`.
The firewall remained intact: fit/audit/test images, public Solar data, GPU/model
training, CSV export, and submission were untouched. A future foreground model
requires separately researched learned contextual features, not scale/threshold
rescue of V40 or assembly of existing detector proposals.
