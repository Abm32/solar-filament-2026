## Mechanistic reading

The learned contextual texture/topology architecture memorized the phenotype only
partially: it recovered roughly half the 308 fit instances (recall ~0.50 at best)
at moderate mask quality (median IoU ~0.585), while emitting hundreds of false
positives (FP 588–684) even on the very images it was overfitting. It cannot
memorize the small/thin filament target on a T4 within the bounded budget, so
there is no basis to expect it to generalize on the full population. This is a
clean architectural negative, consistent with the project's measured recall floor
(union-of-all-detectors 0.8131; 18.7% of tune instances invisible to every
existing proposal source).
