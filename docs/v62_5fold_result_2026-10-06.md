## Against the expectation I wrote down beforehand
I predicted a pooled effect of +0.010 to +0.018 (shrinking the fold-0 headline). The measured
+0.0204 is ABOVE that range. Fold 0 (+0.0204) turned out to be typical, not a lucky fold; the
fold-0 seed-matched replicate (+0.0128) was the low estimate, not the fold-0 seed-42 one. The
seed noise is also much smaller pooled (0.0015) than the single fold-0 pair suggested (0.0049).

## What this establishes and what it does not
- Established: on all 707 files, under clean out-of-fold evaluation, adding a P2 head level with
  stride-2 mask prototypes to the V17 YOLO11s-seg detector improves direct-detector PQ by about
  +0.020, consistently across folds, mainly via small-object recall (+0.049) and better mask IoU
  (+0.008), without more proposals or false positives.
- The gain is NOT in thin filaments (431 GT, +0.002). It is small objects.
- NOT established:
  * the effect after the V11 refiner (the public V17 pipeline), which may absorb part of it;
  * that stride-2 prototypes are the cause (p2last's fold-0 structural fail suggests it, but the
    two variants also differ in initialisation; p2last was not run on folds 1-4);
  * seed-robustness beyond fold 0 (the second seed exists only for fold 0);
  * any public-leaderboard effect. Local gains of this size have previously moved the public score
    by at most one displayed step.

## Decision
The gate authorises BUILDING the matched pipeline, not submitting. Order:
1. Refiner compatibility, measured cleanly: the V11 refiner was trained on the canonical 566 files,
   so it has not seen the 141 canonical held-out (tune+audit) files, which are spread across the
   five OOF folds. On that 141-file subset both the detector (OOF) and the refiner are unseen-data,
   giving a clean baseline+refiner vs p2first+refiner comparison. (The other 566 files would leak.)
2. p2first retrained on all 707 files for the final model.
3. Test export with the same recipe, validated with `scripts/validate_submission.py`.
4. One submission, with the user's go-ahead.
