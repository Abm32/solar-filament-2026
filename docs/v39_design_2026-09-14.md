# V39 design — latent-fragment graph-assembly opportunity gate

Frozen **2026-09-14 before V39 implementation or measurement**.

## Decision and separation from closed work

V39 is a zero-GPU, tune-only opportunity audit for the final ranked clean mechanism
that has not been directly gated: TARDIS-style semantic foreground to skeleton
point cloud to learned pairwise graph assembly.

V16/V17 union analysis selected complete instance proposals and found 212/1,134
tune GT instances missed by all six sources at strict one-to-one IoU >0.5. That
does not establish that those instances are absent from detector output. A long
filament may be represented by several individually sub-threshold masks which a
pairwise graph can reconnect. V38 tested interactions between different GT
filaments; V39 instead tests fragments belonging to one GT filament.

V39 does not train TARDIS, alter V17, read audit/test, export, or submit. It asks
whether enough latent foreground already exists to justify a separately frozen
semantic-and-graph model.

## Data firewall

Allowed:

- hash-pinned official competition training annotations;
- exactly the canonical 94 tune files / 150 annotator records / 1,134 GT;
- all six complete V6/V16/V17 signed proposal-cache populations;
- official polygon masks and expert spines for tune opportunity scoring.

Forbidden:

- the sealed 47-file audit population;
- competition test images or predictions;
- all train, tune, audit, or test image pixels;
- public full-MAGFiLO labels, solar checkpoints, and public payload predictions;
- model training, CSV generation, export, and submission.

The result must record `reads_images=false`, `reads_audit=false`,
`reads_test=false`, `trains=false`, `exports=false`, and `submits=false`.

## Frozen fragment definition

For each GT instance and each grouped proposal from all six complete sources:

1. Rasterize using the existing canonical COCO path.
2. Compute intersection pixels with that GT.
3. A proposal is an **admissible latent fragment** only when:
   - it covers at least **5% of GT area**; and
   - at least **50% of proposal area** lies inside that GT.
4. Deduplicate admissible fragments by exact mask RLE identity across sources, but
   retain the number of distinct sources supporting each fragment.
5. Form the oracle fragment union. This uses tune GT only to measure opportunity;
   it is not a deployable selector or a prediction pipeline.
6. Project the official expert spine into the GT mask using the frozen V31 radius-3
   method. Compute exact union coverage of the projected spine.

An all-source miss is **graph-addressable** only if all hold:

- at least two distinct admissible fragment masks;
- admissible evidence comes from at least two of the six detector sources;
- fragment union covers at least **65% of GT pixels**;
- fragment union covers at least **65% of the projected expert spine**.

The 5% fragment floor removes incidental contacts. The 50% precision floor rejects
masks primarily belonging to another object. The two-fragment/two-source rule
requires genuine assembly evidence rather than relabeling one weak proposal. The
65% area and spine thresholds align the opportunity ceiling with the conservative
IoU 0.65 recovery assumption used by V38.

No threshold sweep is permitted.

## G0 — integrity and regression

Require:

- annotation SHA-256
  `5da9e92b5a1a1947fd5d57adb6688269625c48ec1ef884daf2a01618c9ed54a1`;
- exact tune population 94 files / 150 records / 1,134 GT;
- all six cache populations complete for all 94 tune files;
- recomputed per-source grouped matches exactly
  `841, 794, 826, 884, 843, 893`;
- union matches exactly 922 and misses exactly 212;
- strict one-to-one IoU >0.5 matching;
- synthetic fragment qualification, deduplication, area coverage, and spine
  coverage checks pass;
- deterministic finite output.

Any failure invalidates V39.

## G1 — material graph-opportunity gate

G1 passes only if all hold:

1. at least **65%** of the 212 all-source misses are graph-addressable;
2. perfect recovery of every addressable miss at IoU exactly **0.65**, with zero
   added FP, projects V17 tune PQ to at least **0.50**;
3. median graph-addressable union area coverage is at least **0.75**;
4. median graph-addressable spine coverage is at least **0.75**;
5. all G0 and firewall checks pass.

V17 arithmetic is frozen at IoU sum 481.00931292355114, 704 TP, 306 FP, and 430
FN. The projection is an opportunity ceiling, not model performance.

## Conditional G2

Only passing G1 authorizes a new, separately frozen 16-record numerical overfit
design. It must specify a contamination-safe generic semantic backbone, point
sampling, pair labels, graph assembly, width reconstruction, memory/runtime
limits, exact train PQ/recall/FP gates, and no tune/audit/test access. No TARDIS
package or model is installed or executed by V39 G1.

## Stop rules

- Do not change 5%, 50%, two fragments, two sources, 65% area/spine, 65% miss
  coverage, IoU 0.65, median 0.75, or projected PQ 0.50 after observing results.
- Do not use public MAGFiLO labels or the opaque 0.55 payload.
- Do not reinterpret all proposal foreground as a valid submission ensemble.
- Do not read audit/test, train, export, or submit if G1 fails.
- If G1 fails, close proposal-fragment graph assembly from these six populations.
  A future new path would need a genuinely new semantic foreground generator,
  not graph assembly over the same evidence.

Primary method source: TARDIS, <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11702698/>.

## Final result — closed at G1

The full canonical tune run reproduced source matches
`841/794/826/884/843/893`, union matches 922, and all-source misses 212 exactly.
Only **40/212 (18.8679%)** misses were graph-addressable. Their median union area
coverage was **0.796447** and median projected-spine coverage was **0.788881**, so
the fragment geometry itself passed both 0.75 quality gates. Absolute opportunity
did not: perfect recovery of all 40 at IoU 0.65 with zero added FP projects V17
PQ only from 0.448703 to **0.464294**, and 113/212 misses had zero admissible
fragment.

Therefore `g1_passed=false` and `g2_model_design_authorized=false`. Close graph
assembly over the six existing detector populations. Repeated execution was
byte-identical; result SHA-256 is
`bf224e9de46324f8f7849b7d524f28f9a07d4a5f3fdde6ad370a85d4705a5233`.
The firewall remained intact: no image pixels, audit, test, public Solar data,
training, CSV export, or submission. A future barrier-breaking path must generate
new foreground evidence for the unsupported faint/small instances rather than
rescore or reconnect these proposal pools.
