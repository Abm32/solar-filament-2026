## Hypothesis

V17 remains recall-limited: 212/1,134 tune instances are missed by every measured YOLO proposal pool. EdgeAttNet reports a solar-specific preprocessing chain—disk localization, radial brightness flattening, mild Gaussian smoothing, and CLAHE—intended to expose faint filament structure under limb darkening and site-dependent contrast. The hypothesis is that applying this chain consistently at detector train and inference time recovers genuinely new proposals. The V17 refiner continues to read original raw pixels, isolating preprocessing to proposal discovery.

Primary references:

- Solomon et al., *EdgeAttNet: Towards Barb-Aware Filament Segmentation*, 2025: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02964>.
- Men'shchikov, *Background derivation and image flattening: getimages*, A&A 2017.
- Reza, *CLAHE for real-time image enhancement*, 2004.
- Ahmadzadeh et al., *MAGFiLO*, Scientific Data 2024.

These sources support the mechanism, not a competition-PQ gain. EdgeAttNet uses semantic metrics and complete MAGFiLO; V23 reimplements only the transform on approved competition images.

## Frozen transform

For every 2048x2048 grayscale competition image:

1. Gaussian blur 5x5, Otsu threshold, largest external component, minimum-enclosing circle.
2. Require disk center within 6% of frame center, radius 35-55% of width, and disk fraction 0.45-0.80. A label-blind scan of all 707 competition images found center-offset p99 74.68 px and one valid GONG pointing outlier at 112.36 px; 6% (122.88 px) includes the observed population without changing disk estimates.
3. Within radius <=0.98, area-downsample the disk to 512x512, compute a 256-bin radial median intensity profile, fill missing bins by interpolation, and smooth the profile with a 15-bin Gaussian kernel. Interpolate that profile back at native resolution.
4. Divide image intensity by the radial background profile and multiply by the median in-disk background.
5. Robustly scale in-disk flattened pixels from p2-p98 to uint8.
6. Gaussian blur 3x3, sigma 0.7.
7. CLAHE `clipLimit=2.0`, `tileGridSize=(8,8)`.
8. Set off-disk pixels to zero; replicate grayscale to three JPEG channels at quality 95.

No parameter is selected from labels. The same deterministic transform is used for train, tune, audit, and—only if export is authorized—test.
