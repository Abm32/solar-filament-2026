# V11: proposal-aware refiner continuation

Status: **run COMPLETE; promotion gate FAILED; candidate exported separately and
submitted for measurement, scoring 0.39 public — a new best.** The gate verdict
stands: V11 is **not promoted**. See "Measured result" and "Public measurement"

## Frozen experiment

Keep the pinned V6 detector, COCO refiner, 566 training filenames, 94 tuning
filenames, and 47 audit filenames. Keep crop size 256, detector resolution 1024,
four detector and four crop flips, confidence 0.40, mask threshold 0.40,
confidence-ordered pixel ownership, prediction minimum area 20, and canonical
COCO targets for every annotator and category. The audit was involved in earlier
checkpoint selection; it is a historical check, not an untouched holdout.

Compare the frozen refiner against three continuations starting from the exact
same canonical checkpoint: (1) GT-centered crops with the shared bounded jitter,
(2) detector-proposal-centered
crops with the original three image channels, and (3) the same proposal crops
with a fourth proposal-mask channel. Each arm sees the same matched annotation
cohort once per epoch, the same deterministic sample/augmentation schedule,
batch size 16, four epochs, AdamW learning rate 5e-5, weight decay 1e-4, cosine
decay over four epochs, and equal 0.5 BCE + 0.5 soft Dice loss. Every epoch is
evaluated by final-image global tuning PQ. The four conditioned checkpoints are
also evaluated with full-crop mask growth, using the same learned weights.
This is **12 trained checkpoints, 16 checkpoint/mode evaluations, plus one
frozen control**, with no threshold or epoch-budget expansion after outcomes.

All three continuations use FP32, with autocast and gradient scaling disabled.
This common precision choice was fixed before outcomes and differs from the
historical refiner's AMP continuation. Every batch therefore performs one
optimizer update; scale-overflow skips cannot break matched update budgets.
The GPU smoke validates batch-16 memory feasibility. The disabled scaler's
empty state is retained in checkpoints for uniform resume handling.

Generate training proposals only for the 566 authorized training filenames.
These are in-sample V6 detections, an explicitly optimistic first screen;
fresh folds excluding those images from detector training are required before
claiming independent generalization. No detector runs on validation or test:
use all 321 hash-pinned frozen raw caches. Never use a GT seed at inference.

For each annotator separately, keep at most one proposal per annotation. A
proposal is eligible when its best target IoU is at least 0.30, its second-best
same-annotator IoU is below 0.20, and the best/second margin is at least 0.15.
Choose the eligible proposal with greatest IoU, then confidence, then original
order. This deliberately excludes ambiguous and unmatched proposals rather
than treating incompletely annotated regions as negative examples. Keep each
annotator's target separate; do not union masks or drop chirality categories.
The GT-centered control uses exactly that matched cohort, avoiding annotation
weighting by duplicate-proposal count.

Apply identical bounded crop jitter to every arm: context-square scale
0.9–1.1 and center offsets up to 10% of its original side. Crop image, actual
proposal seed, and the single canonical target from those native-image bounds;
use bilinear image and nearest-neighbor mask resize. If jitter removes all of
the target or seed, fall back to the original bounds. Rotate/flip all three
together; apply gamma/brightness only to the image. The fourth convolution
channel begins at zero, with normalization mean 0/std 1; old RGB weights and
normalization are preserved. GPU smoke must verify initial logit equivalence
within declared float tolerances and nonzero fourth-channel loss gradients.
Every scheduled training crop is validated before any arm trains. If nearest-
neighbor resize erases a seed or target, stop before training; never silently
discard a sample or change one arm's budget. This is a matched jittered
GT-centered continuation control, alongside the unchanged frozen control.

## Selection and export gate

Select the greatest tuning PQ from the predeclared set; numerical ties within
1e-12 prefer the frozen control, then GT-centered, then unconditioned proposal,
then conditioned constrained, then conditioned full crop, then earlier epoch.
Write the frozen selection before auditing. Only that winner and the frozen
control receive one audit pass. Audit does not choose another candidate.

Promotion requires **at least +0.01 tuning PQ over frozen V6**, no worse than
recorded V10 tuning PQ, and nonnegative audit deltas versus **both** frozen V6
and recorded V10. V10 references are derived from the hash-pinned diagnostic
CSV: tuning counts (IoU sum 430.3746937713222, TP 632, FP 217, FN 502), audit
(184.34953270420428, 267, 90, 192). Source SHA-256:
`8a334947ee5eb5876b618a1f00e23b023af2e2f8ed4be22b62067f3af4b8375a`.
These gates are conservative screening heuristics, not significance claims.

Frozen-control per-annotator counts must match the original diagnostics to
absolute tolerance 1e-10 on every evaluated file. Its probability inference
retains every proposal in original batch-eight order before confidence cuts.
An eligible export additionally requires exact frozen-control submission bytes
and independent CSV structural validation. Failed gates produce no candidate
submission. This program never uploads a Kaggle submission.

## Reproducibility and smoke

`scripts/prepare_v11_refiner.py` stages isolated shared modules, source hashes,
321 raw-cache hashes, reference counts, metadata, and a predeclared protocol.
Smoke and full runs use separate Kaggle notebook IDs. Smoke uses a few training
and tuning files, one epoch, and bounded sample count; it performs no audit,
test inference, or export and can never be promoted. Full settings cannot be
silently changed by smoke flags.

The runtime validates split, annotation/checkpoint/source identities, raw-cache
coverage and hashes, and library versions before training. Training cache
signatures include source image hashes and are separate from frozen caches.
Epoch checkpoints retain optimizer/scheduler/scaler and RNG states; immutable
run identity prevents resume with another script or cohort. JSON manifests,
per-annotator PQ diagnostics, training losses, sample/update counts, checkpoint
hashes, and selection decisions are saved as the run progresses.

Run source/data hashes and smoke results will be appended after verification.

Local verification: 67 tests passed across V11 geometry/protocol/staging,
existing V10, and detector TTA. The V11 checks cover train/evaluation isolation,
per-annotator positive matching and ambiguity, real-seed spatial transforms,
thin-mask failure before training, identical sample/update budgets, all-group
batching before confidence cuts, ownership, selection/promotion gates, and
actual staging against local pinned data. GPU numerical and smoke validation
remain required; no new model score is claimed by these CPU checks.

### Interpretation

Instance conditioning, the primary V11 hypothesis, **failed**. The proposal-seed
channel was the weakest arm at every epoch, and its epochs 2 and 3 scored below
the frozen control. The predeclared full-context-crop variant added only about
+0.0002 over its constrained counterpart, so permitting mask growth did not
recover the 89 geometrically unreachable instances. The seed mechanics were
verified sound, so this is a modeling result and not a plumbing defect:
conditioning on a noisy detector seed appears to teach the refiner to trust it.

Proposal-centered training beat the matched GT-centered control by only +0.00069
tuning PQ. Both gained roughly +0.0045 relative to the frozen control, so nearly
all of the movement comes from continuing to train the refiner at all, not from
the crop distribution. Per-epoch scores are non-monotonic in all three arms
(epoch 1 high, epochs 2-3 lower, epoch 4 high), which is consistent with these
differences being close to run-to-run noise.

The selected candidate changes tuning counts by +3 TP and -5 FP against control.
Matched-mask geometry is essentially unchanged, and the 278 proposals lost at the
consensus cutoff are still lost. Combined with V10's paired intervals including
zero, the refiner-side frozen-detector track now looks saturated at sub-0.01
local movement. The next experiments should be the proposal-quality/IoU head or
the V12 detector-resolution comparison.

**These interpretations were written before the public measurement below and are
retained unedited.** The public result partly contradicts the saturation reading:
see the reassessment at the end.

### Reassessment

The 0.39 result is genuine evidence that the refiner track is **not** as saturated
as the local numbers suggested. Three qualifications matter:

1. **The gate verdict is unchanged.** V11 achieved +0.005177 tuning PQ against a
   required +0.01. A favourable public result does not retroactively pass a
   predeclared screen, and 0.39 is a single rounded observation. V11 remains
   unpromoted; the accepted baseline for further frozen-weight work is still the
   established pipeline.
2. **The +0.01 threshold now looks miscalibrated.** V10 gained +0.0012 tuning PQ
   and returned 0.38; V11 gained +0.005177 and returned 0.39. Two points do not
   establish a transfer function, but they are consistent with public score
   responding to local gains well below +0.01. Future gates should be recalibrated
   against this evidence rather than kept at a threshold that would have rejected
   the only improvement we have measured.
3. **What actually earned the gain is still ambiguous.** The winning arm differs
   from the matched GT-centered control by only +0.00069 tuning PQ, so the
   proposal-centered crop distribution is not clearly the cause. Roughly +0.0045
   of the movement comes from simply continuing to train the refiner for four
   more epochs. A cheap, informative follow-up is to export and measure
   `gt_centered_e4` from the same run: if it also scores 0.39, the gain is
   continuation training, not proposal centering. Both checkpoints already exist,
   so this costs one export run and one submission, no training.

The instance-conditioning conclusion is unaffected: the seed-channel arm was the
weakest at every epoch and should not be carried forward. V12's detector-resolution
comparison also remains well motivated, since recall and mask geometry are still
the measured bottleneck.

### Verdict

**The V11 public gain is continuation training, not proposal centering.** The two
arms tie on the public leaderboard, and the arm with the *lower* local tuning PQ
matched the selected one. The crop distribution is not the lever; continuing to
train the refiner for four more epochs from the COCO checkpoint is.

This means **both** of V11's substantive hypotheses now lack support. Instance
conditioning was the weakest arm at every epoch, and proposal centering is
indistinguishable from its GT-centered control publicly. The part that worked was
incidental to the experiment's design.

Three consequences for planning:

1. **The refiner track is less saturated than the local numbers implied.** Cheap
   continuation headroom exists; the earlier "saturated at sub-0.01 local movement"
   reading is further weakened. But note both arms sit at the same rounded 0.39, so
   this is not evidence that more epochs keep paying — only that the first four did.
2. **Two-decimal public scoring cannot resolve ~0.0007 local deltas.** Do not spend
   further submissions separating arms this close. Submissions are better spent on
   changes with materially different mechanisms.
3. **The calibration set is now three points:** +0.0012 → 0.38, +0.0045 → 0.39,
   +0.0052 → 0.39. Local gains around +0.004–0.005 do appear to transfer. This
   confirms +0.01 was a miscalibrated screen, without licensing acceptance of noise.

The V12 detector-resolution comparison and the E-C learned proposal-quality head
remain the two substantive next bets: recall is still the measured bottleneck, and
neither of V11's rejected mechanisms touched the 278 cutoff-lost or 228
missing-raw instances.
