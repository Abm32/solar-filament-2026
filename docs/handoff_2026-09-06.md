  detector/refiner training**, and no rerun of detector inference.
- Preserves our four-view `Solar Filament Detector TTA` consensus and crop TTA.
- V6 mask threshold/consensus cutoff stay 0.40/0.40. Order overlaps by detector
  consensus times mean refiner foreground output; acceptance uses raw consensus.
  Empty refinement has zero foreground ordering score; original-proposal
  support is stored separately. This description was corrected on 6 September
  against the frozen source and manifest; submission bytes are unchanged.
- Adds only V8 rescues with mask threshold 0.45, consensus >=0.40, >=3 views,
  original-proposal support >=0.50, nonempty refinement, and >=90% residual area.
  Reject duplicates at IoU >=0.50 or intersection/smaller-area >=0.80.
  V8 additions cannot erase accepted V6 pixels; minimum area remains 20 pixels.
- Tested 9 eligible variants from a maximum predeclared grid of 15; the three
  rejection-only gates made no difference. Ranking and rescue supplied the gain.
- Selected on 94 tuning files first, then audited only that candidate/control
  on 47 files. No post-audit reselection or grid expansion.
