## Finding
TTA-merge monotonically HURTS: each added flipped view injects sub-pixel-
misaligned duplicate masks that the IoU>=0.5 merge fails to collapse, inflating FP
(1 -> 68 -> 124) and slightly blurring boundaries (medIoU 0.781 -> 0.770 -> 0.752).
The no-TTA native-2048 yolo11s-seg model reproduced V42 exactly (PQ 0.768, medIoU
0.781, FP 1) and remains the best configuration.

## Gate decision
g2_passed=false. The mask-IoU ceiling (~0.78 matched IoU) is a genuine YOLO-seg
mask-head limitation for pixel-thin filaments; averaging views cannot fix boundary
precision. Do NOT pursue TTA-merge further. The banked breakthrough stands: V42
broke the recall wall (recall 0.997, FP 1) that closed V38-V41, moving the
bottleneck from detection to mask boundary precision.
