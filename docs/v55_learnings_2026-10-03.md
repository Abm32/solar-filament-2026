loss trajectories with targeted literature. Purpose: deduce *why* the variants behave
as they do and derive concrete, grounded improvements rather than guess the next run.

- **clDice-w1.0 (tv73, tv82): plateau-then-collapse.** Tversky frozen ~0.99 for ~35

  dominates the gradient and starves the Tversky signal.

## 2. Research — what the literature says (and where our setup is wrong)

### a) clDice is an AUXILIARY term; weight it SMALL (not 2.0)
clDice paper (Shit et al., CVPR 2021, arXiv 2003.07311) combines it as
`(1−α)·softDice + α·clDice`, α ≈ 0.3–0.5 — never dominant. Centerline-Boundary-Dice
(arXiv 2407.01517) warns clDice+Dice causes "diameter imbalance, favoring larger
vessels" when over-weighted. => **clDice-w2.0 is the documented failure mode.** The two
cl2 variants are expected to under-perform; the w1.0 variants are already at the high end
of the recommended auxiliary range. CONCLUSION: do not exceed clDice-w ~1.0; sweet spot
likely 0.3–0.5 (consistent with V52's own best being w0.5/w1.0).

### b) The plateau is a known Tversky+focal pathology — fixable
Focal Tversky paper (Abraham & Khan, arXiv 1810.07842) documents exactly our curve:
the focal exponent γ **over-suppresses the loss near high Tversky index** (flat gradient),
"usually as the model is close to convergence" — and more generally when another term
dominates, the Tversky gradient is too weak to escape the all-background saddle. Their
fix: **supervise the output layer with PLAIN Tversky (γ=1), not focal**, to keep a strong
error signal; keep γ ∈ [1, 4/3] (they found γ=4/3 best, NOT 2.0 as I used). CONCLUSION:
my γ=2.0 is too aggressive and contributes to the long plateau; use γ=1.0–1.33, and/or
drop focal on the region term entirely.

### c) My α/β is unconventional for small structures (but correct for OUR goal)
Both Tversky papers (1706.05721, 1810.07842) define α=FP weight, β=FN weight and
recommend **α=0.3, β=0.7** (i.e. penalise FN harder) to raise *recall* for small/thin
ROIs — the opposite of what I did (α>β for precision). This is deliberate and correct
for V55's goal: V52's fusion deficiency is PRECISION (519 FP), not recall (it already
recovers 93 FNs). But it means I'm fighting the small-structure imbalance that normally
starves recall — which likely explains the very long plateau for the w1.0 runs too:
a high FP penalty on a thin-structure problem makes "predict almost nothing" a deep
early attractor. CONCLUSION: a moderate α (0.6–0.65) likely converges faster and keeps
more recall than α0.8; tv73 (α0.7) and tv82 (α0.8) will show this on tune eval.

### d) Topology losses are setting-dependent, and OUR labels are noisy
TopoMortar (arXiv 2503.03365, 2026): clDice best for topology *in general* but the
"relative advantageousness depends on the experimental setting"; **Skeleton-Recall loss
is best under NOISY LABELS**. "Does the Skeleton-Recall Loss Really Work?" (arXiv
2508.11374, 2026) questions skeleton losses outright. MAGFiLO has a known ~18.7%
ambiguous/invisible-filament annotation bucket (project §4) = a noisy-label regime.
CONCLUSION: our heavy reliance on soft-clDice may be mis-matched to noisy GT; a
Skeleton-Recall-style term (cheaper, noise-robust) is a worth-trying alternative to clDice.

## 3. What this predicts for the current V55 runs (to verify at tune eval)
1. **cl2 variants (tv64_cl2, tv82_cl2) will under-perform** — stuck optimisation; likely
   low standalone PQ and poor FP reduction. Probably discard.
2. **tv73 / tv82 are the real candidates.** Expect LOWER FP than V52's base32 (519) /
   w1 (364) because α>β actively penalises FP — this is the whole point. The risk is
   recall loss; α0.8 (tv82) may over-cut recall vs α0.7 (tv73). The best fusion source is
   whichever hits the lowest FP while keeping recall high enough to still recover a chunk
   of V17's 317 FNs.
3. If tv73/tv82 cut FP meaningfully (say <300 at matched recall), the V53 fusion recall
   path (beta>0) that failed before could finally be net-positive. That is the gate test.

## 4. V56 design (grounded, if V55 tune eval is promising-but-not-passing)
- **Loss:** `0.5·softDice-Tversky(α0.65,β0.35) + 0.5·clDice`, γ=1.0 on the region term
  (drop focal exponent that caused over-suppression), OR focal γ=4/3 ONLY on intermediate
  supervision with plain Tversky at the output (Abraham & Khan's fix).
- **clDice-w ≤ 0.5** (back to the auxiliary range; abandon w2.0).
- **Warm-start the region term:** 10–15 epochs of plain Dice (α=β=0.5) BEFORE engaging
  the FP-penalty, to escape the all-background saddle fast, then switch to α>β. Directly
  attacks the 35-epoch plateau.
- **Resolution:** try res 1536–2048 for a precision run (thin-structure boundaries) once
  the loss schedule is fixed — but only if a 1024 variant already beats V52 on FP.
- **Alternative term:** a Skeleton-Recall-style loss (noise-robust) instead of clDice,
  given MAGFiLO's noisy ~18.7% bucket.
