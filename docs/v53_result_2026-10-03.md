# V53 — V17 + V52 (clDice semantic) fusion: NEGATIVE (gate failed, but first positive delta)

Date: 2026-10-03. VM: rogii-gpu (4× T4). Firewall intact: no tune labels in V52
training; audit sealed; no test inference / CSV / submission.

## Question
V51 (fusing V44, a YOLO11-seg model from V17's own family) failed because V44 was
*nested inside* V17 — correlated FPs, nothing orthogonal to add. V52 is a different
architecture (compact pure-torch U-Net) with a topology-preserving objective
(soft-clDice, arXiv 2003.07311). Does fusing it into the exact V17 pipeline beat
V17's tune PQ 0.448703 by the predeclared +0.010 gate?

## Pre-flight diagnostic (scripts/diagnose_v52_v17_overlap.py, 94-file fusion-tune)
First measure whether V52's errors are decorrelated from V17 before spending the grid.

| source | tp | fp | fn | FP-corr w/ V17 | recovers V17 FNs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| V17 (seed1/mr1_iou030 → V11 → t0.45_c0.30) | 479 | 171 | 317 | — | — |
| V52 w1_t060 (clDice-w1 @0.60) | 446 | 364 | 350 | 24.2% | 56/317 (17.7%) |
| V52 base32_t040 (base @0.40) | 501 | 519 | 295 | 18.7% | 93/317 (29.3%) |

**V52 is the first genuinely orthogonal source found.** Only 19–24% of its FPs land
on V17's FPs (vs the YOLO family's near-nesting), and base32_t040 recovers 93 of the
317 GT instances V17 misses. The diagnostic said the grid was worth running — unlike
V51, where the mechanism was absent.

## Fusion grid (scripts/v53_fusion.py, 756 candidates)
extra_set {w1_t060, base32_t040} × alpha {0,.25,.5} × beta {0,.5,1} × V17's 6×7 t/c.
- alpha: V17 score → (1−a)·s17 + a·e, e = best matching (IoU≥0.5) V52 score  [agreement]
- beta: unmatched V52 proposals added with score β·e  [recall]
- Control a0_b0 (per set) reproduced V50/V17 **exactly** at all 42 t/c cells →
  `control_verified=True` (firewall anchor holds; harness trustworthy).

### Result: gate FAILED
- **Selected: `base32_t040/a0.25_b0.00/t0.45_c0.40` → PQ 0.449278** (tp675/fp249/fn459).
- Delta vs V17 = **+0.00058**. Promotion bar +0.010 → `audit_authorized=False`.
  Audit stays sealed; no test/CSV/submission.

Monotone and decisive:
1. **beta (recall) is strictly harmful**: b0 > b0.5 > b1.0 at every (set, alpha).
   Adding V52's unmatched proposals floods FPs faster than it recovers TPs — PQ falls
   to 0.38–0.41 at b1.0. The 93 recoverable FNs are not separable from V52's 519 FPs
   by score alone.
2. **alpha=0.25 helps only at beta=0, only for the high-recall set**: up-weighting
   V17 proposals that V52 *agrees with* nudges a few borderline proposals past the
   confidence gate (fp 306→249 while giving back some tp). alpha=0.5 over-trusts V52
   and loses TP (704→643). Net best gain +0.00058.

## Conclusion
V52's orthogonality is real (diagnostic confirmed), and it produced the **first
positive fusion delta** in the project — but +0.0006 is an order of magnitude below
the +0.010 gate and squarely inside the "local gains that never move public" band
(ledger E024). The gain comes only from precision-preserving agreement; the lever
with headroom (recall via V52's unique TPs) is swamped by its FP rate.

Reconfirms the root wall (§4): the hard-FN bucket (~317 genuinely faint filaments)
cannot be recovered without importing more FPs than TPs, even from an orthogonal
model. **Keep V17 `56103401` at public 0.39.**

Evidence: `outputs/v53_fusion/tune_merged.json`, `outputs/v53_diagnostic/{w1_t060,base32_t040}.json`.

## What this leaves (next levers, by remaining EV)
The "add an orthogonal source" path is now characterized, not just closed: the gating
problem is FP-vs-TP separability of the recovered instances, not source correlation.
Candidates that attack *that*:
1. **Score-recalibrate V52's unmatched proposals** before the beta-add: the mean-prob
   score is a poor FP/TP separator. A cheap per-proposal feature (compactness,
   skeleton length, mean prob × area) + a logistic gate fit on FIT only could make
   beta>0 net-positive. Low cost (reuses existing dumps + V17 cache), untouched.
2. **Precision-focused V52 retrain** (higher clDice-w already helped: w1 fp364 <
   base fp519): push further — FN-weighted BCE, or train at full 2048 to sharpen thin
   structures and cut the FP rate directly.
3. Agreement-only ensemble of V52 variants (w1 ∩ base) as a cleaner extra source
   (intersection should cut FP while keeping the shared TP core).
