## Actual predeclared grid

Fixed control: canonical COCO targets, the same 94 tuning / 47 audit filenames,
1024px cached detector input, four-view detector consensus and crop averaging,
V6 mask threshold 0.40 and detector-consensus cutoff 0.40. Fresh score-ordered
non-overlap cleanup starts from untrimmed masks, retaining the existing area rules.

`P` is the original anchor proposal, `R` the constrained refined mask, and `p`
the resized four-view-averaged refiner output. `qP = mean(p[P])`. In the actual
`make_record`, `untrimmed` is `R` when nonempty, otherwise the fallback proposal
`P`. Stored `foreground_probability = mean(p[untrimmed])` is computed after
that fallback choice and before non-overlap cleanup. Thus `quality_rank` uses
`mean(p[R])` for nonempty refinement but **`qP` for fallback**, not zero merely
because `R` is empty. This differs from the original research proposal's
pre-fallback `qF` definition; it documents the frozen implementation without
changing code or the grid.

| QUALITY value | V6 change | RESCUE choices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `none` | Exact control behavior | `none`, `rescue35`, `rescue40` |
| `drop_empty` | Reject empty refinement instead of restoring detector mask | Same three |
| `proposal50` | Require original-proposal support `qP >= 0.50` | Same three |
| `views3` | Require at least three distinct detector views | Same three |
| `quality_rank` | Order by detector consensus × `foreground_probability` (fallback: `qP`); keep original 0.40 acceptance cutoff | Same three |

This is **15 maximum**, not 15 unconditional trials: seven initial candidates
(control, four isolated quality variants, two isolated rescues), then up to
eight combinations. Each combination is eligible only if both corresponding
isolated variants improve tuning PQ by more than `1e-12`. Tuning PQ selects the
winner; ties within `1e-12` favor fewer interventions/control.

`rescue35` / `rescue40` use V8 consensus cutoffs 0.35 / 0.40, mask threshold
0.45, at least three distinct views, `qP >= 0.50`, and no empty refinement.
V6 owns pixels first. In V8-confidence order, reject a rescue if mask IoU is
at least 0.50 or intersection/minimum-mask-area is at least 0.80 against V6
or an earlier rescue. Subtract occupied pixels only from the rescue; require
at least 90% of its untrimmed refined area and the existing 20-pixel minimum.
No equal-weight detector fusion or alteration of accepted V6 masks.

The [research note](v10_research_2026-09-05.md) originally recommended six
candidates. Its appended execution note records the actual predeclared expansion
(quality ranking and a second rescue cutoff); the original recommendation is
preserved. Do not expand this grid after inspecting full-run outcomes.

## Research basis and limitations

References were already consulted in the research sidecar; no new network
research was performed in this documentation handoff.

- Mask quality and classification confidence differ; a trained IoU head is not
  our mean-output heuristic. [Mask Scoring R-CNN](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.00241)
- Foreground-mean score multiplication has an official implementation precedent,
  not a calibration guarantee for our refiner. [Mask2Former `instance_inference`](https://github.com/facebookresearch/Mask2Former/blob/main/mask2former/maskformer_model.py#L314-L345)
- PQ depends on final instance masks and unmatched objects; ranking here can
  matter through pixel ownership, not an AP ranking curve. [Panoptic Segmentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.00868)
- Missing annotations can corrupt hard-negative supervision. Proposal-aware
  training remains deferred and requires verified negatives. [Soft Sampling](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.06986)

Quality/support scores are uncalibrated proxies; flips are correlated, and the
33 V8-only raw tuning opportunities are not guaranteed recoverable detections.
The audit was used in earlier V6 checkpoint selection, so it is not untouched.
No learned scorer is fitted on tuning/audit data. Keep public 0.38 as the best
until a genuinely higher public result is measured. Proposal-aware refiner
training is **deferred pending candidate/public evidence**; this run used frozen
weights and trained neither a refiner nor a scorer. The immediate handoff is
the validated, unsubmitted candidate, not another training launch.
