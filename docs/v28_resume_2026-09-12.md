## Learnability decision

Detector confidence is the mandatory baseline. The candidate score is `confidence × predicted_utility`.

V28 may proceed only if `oof_report.json` sets `learnable=true`, requiring all three:

1. candidate TP-vs-FP AUC > confidence AUC by **0.01**;
2. candidate Spearman utility correlation > confidence by **0.01**;
3. file-cross-fitted expected-PQ surrogate > confidence by **0.005**.

If any fail, close the utility scorer **without tune/audit/test access**. Do not weaken gates, add epochs, alter folds, or select a different score after seeing OOF.

If all pass, freeze a separate V28 implementation/design before any tune inference. Do not treat feasibility OOF as submission authorization.

## Target and serving population

The cohort uses exactly the V26 hybrid proposal population on the 566 frozen training files:

- V17 detector checkpoint SHA-256:
  `ae476e663039fbbbfda77ec519635ca15e2d50d1fe9f1af54735f84dbdc251ef`;
- V26 full + 3×3 native tile geometry;
- `iou030`, four flip views, detector floor 0.10;
- nearest-center tile ownership and mask NMS 0.50;
- exact V17 refiner, each proposal refined independently at threshold 0.45 before final ownership;
- every unmatched proposal retained as a negative.

Per-proposal target:

- for each annotator record, best refined-mask IoU against that record;
- threshold IoU strictly at >0.5;
- `utility` = annotator-average of `IoU × 1[IoU>0.5]`;
- `positive_fraction` = fraction of annotators for which the proposal matches;
- detector confidence remains an input and baseline, not blindly replaced.

Crop channels are raw image, proposal mask, independently refined mask, and refiner probability.

## Why this differs from V14/V15

- V14 trained the IoU head on positive-only proposals and produced a near-constant score; its diagnostics were also misaligned.
- V15 fixed alignment/negatives but its IoU head AUC was 0.688 versus detector confidence 0.872, and final PQ regressed.
- V28 trains and evaluates on the exact V26 serving proposal population, includes all unmatched negatives, reads predicted mask + image features, uses physical-file OOF, and must beat confidence before tune can open.
