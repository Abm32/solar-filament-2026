# V17 design — the `mask_ratio=1` detector inside V13's winning recipe

Written **before** the full run, 8 September 2026, after E026 resolved V16's two open
questions. This run **trains nothing**.

## The gap this exploits

V16 trained a genuinely better detector and then measured it with the wrong recipe.

Look at what V16 actually evaluated: **one** candidate per arm, `t0.40/c0.40`, at the
Ultralytics **default box `iou=0.7`**, using the **canonical (frozen)** refiner. Compare
that to the configuration that produced this project's best public score:

| | detector | box `iou` | refiner | candidates swept |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| V13 (public 0.39, best measured) | frozen V6, `mask_ratio=2` | **0.3** | **V11 `proposal_centered_e4`** | **42** |
| V16 (`arm_r1`) | **`mask_ratio=1`** | 0.7 | canonical (frozen) | **1** |
| **V17 (this run)** | **`mask_ratio=1`** | **{0.3, 0.7} swept** | **V11 `proposal_centered_e4`** | **84** |

V16 never swept a grid, never applied `iou=0.3`, and never used the V11 refiner. It
therefore never combined its better detector with **any** part of the recipe that
actually scores 0.39. That composition is what V17 measures.

## Why this is not just optimism

E026 already measured the pieces, at zero GPU cost, and they point the same way.

**The detector improvement replicated across two independent training seeds** on the
mechanistic pre-refiner metric: raw proposal IoU mean +0.0109 / +0.0153 and max
+0.0110 / +0.0084 versus the frozen control, breaking `ceiling_analysis` §4's 0.8734
cap in both (0.8844, 0.8818).

**The two levers are not redundant.** Grouped pre-refiner, tune group, same files and GT:

| source | recall | meanIoU | oracle PQ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A — frozen V6 @ `iou=0.7` | 0.7416 | 0.6371 | 0.5426 |
| B — frozen V6 @ `iou=0.3` (V13's lever) | 0.7002 | 0.6366 | 0.5243 |
| **C — `arm_r1` @ `iou=0.7`** | **0.7795** | **0.6442** | **0.5644** |

C > A > B on all three axes, so `mask_ratio=1` dominates the lever already shipped
rather than duplicating it. Stacking them was the obvious untested move.

**The smoke test then confirmed it before the full run.** `--limit` narrows the grid,
cells and seeds but still evaluates all 141 validation files, so its PQ figures are real:
`mr1_iou030/t0.40_c0.30` scored **tune 0.448172 / audit 0.470022** against V13's
0.441777 / 0.464644, and its raw proposal population (mean **0.5692**, max **0.9119**)
beats *both* parents — control 0.5253/0.8734 and `arm_r1`@`iou=0.7` 0.5362/0.8844.
`mask_ratio=1` and `iou=0.3` compose at the proposal stage exactly as E026 predicted.

## Why inference-only is the point, not a shortcut

E026's other finding was a **detector-training** noise floor: re-running V16's training
with an unchanged configuration moved tune PQ by up to 0.0077, several times any
arm-vs-control effect in that experiment. That is why V16's end-to-end deltas flipped
sign between seeds and could not be interpreted at all.

V17 trains nothing. Both `arm_r1` checkpoints already exist on disk and are pinned by
SHA-256, as are the refiner and every cached proposal. **The noise floor that made V16
uninterpretable does not apply to this run**, and the comparison is deterministic and
exactly reproducible. Any V17 that retrained the detector would have re-inherited that
±0.007 floor and needed multiple seeds to say anything; this one does not.

## Protocol, fixed before any full-run number is observed

- **Frozen control first**, as in V12/V13/V15/V16: frozen V6 detector, the 321 frozen
  `iou=0.7` caches, canonical refiner. Must reproduce E015's exact counts (tune
  `[426.5473399071962, 626, 211, 508]`, audit `[182.75261599994462, 265, 88, 194]`) and
  its exported CSV must hash to the accepted 0.38 file
  `bf11690e9bcce73b0c627096cc1198d9fb0916650f7fa4b3f08f1167bb76bde9`. Abort otherwise.
- **84 predeclared candidates**: 2 box-`iou` cells x 6 thresholds x 7 confidences, bound
  at runtime to `detector_tta.LOCAL_*` so the grid cannot silently widen. Box `iou` is a
  *declared axis* rather than a fixed choice because E026 measured the two levers as
  non-redundant at *different* `iou` values, leaving the best pairing genuinely open.
- **Selection is tune-PQ only**, on the pre-registered primary seed (seed 1). Audit never
  selects. Seed 2 is evaluated as corroboration and can never influence selection.
- **SUBMIT_BAR** (authorises exporting a CSV): tune >= **0.441777** (V13) and audit >=
  **0.450130** (E015 floor).
- **PROMOTION_GATE** (authorises calling it a real gain): tune >= V13 + **0.010** and
  audit >= **0.464644**. Per E024, a +0.003–0.010 local gain moved the public score not
  at all, so anything smaller is a measurement, not a promotion.
- **Cache identity spans `detector_sha | mask_ratio | cell | split | name`**, so the five
  proposal populations now in play (V6@0.7, V6@0.3, and `arm_r1`@{0.3,0.7} per seed)
  cannot collide by construction.
- Nothing trains; nothing submits. Submission stays a separate manual step.

## Honest expectation

The smoke test's +0.0064 tune over V13 clears SUBMIT_BAR but **not** the +0.010
promotion gate, and the full sweep may well land in the same band. E024 is the
cautionary precedent: V13 cleared both its bars, beat all three parents, and the public
score stayed 0.39 — local gains in the +0.003–0.010 range are invisible at two public
decimals.

So the realistic outcome is a candidate that is *authorised for measurement* rather than
a demonstrated public gain, and it should be reported that way. What makes it worth one
submission anyway: V17 is the first candidate whose advantage is grounded in a
**replicated, mechanistic** improvement in proposal quality rather than another
inference-side rearrangement, and it costs no training. If the full sweep finds a
`mask_ratio=1` cell materially above the smoke point, the case strengthens; if it lands
at +0.006, the submission is a calibration point on whether a proposal-stage gain
transfers where inference-stage gains did not.
