## Decision

Do not lower the 0.97 gate after observation, alter seed thresholds, change bin
count/alpha, repair selected failure records, drop the DT control, proceed to the
overfit gate, or train V31. The target oracle exists precisely to prevent GPU
spend when the representation/decoder contract cannot reconstruct its source
labels.

The near-pass is still important mechanistic evidence: official expert spines
produce a highly faithful global instance representation, while standard DT is
an invalid matched control for these morphologies under the frozen decoder.
Any future spine study requires a separately researched and frozen control and
decoder, not a V31 rescue.

boundary/distance/flow with suppressed Euler integration. It must begin from a
