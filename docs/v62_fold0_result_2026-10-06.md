## What this does and does not show
- The effect is reproduced across two seeds for p2first on fold 0, with no proposal/FP inflation,
  and it shows up as better SMALL-object recall (small 0.390 -> 0.440 at the cut, pool 0.564 ->
  0.644) and better mask overlap (mean matched IoU 0.6444 -> 0.6521).
- NOT a result yet: one fold (141 files). Averaging the two seed pairs gives roughly +0.018, below
  the seed-42-only +0.023; the 5-fold run is what decides promotion.
- Thin recall did NOT improve (0.616 -> 0.581 at the cut; pool 0.698 -> 0.674, n = 86 GT), so the
  gain is in small objects, not thin ones. Noted, not interpreted at n = 86.
- Attribution is partial. p2last (P2 detection, stride-4 masks) did not pass structurally while
  p2first (P2 detection + stride-2 prototypes) did. The simplest reading is that the stride-2 mask
  prototypes, not the extra detection scale, carry the small-object gain (small objects are only
  a few pixels at stride-4 mask resolution, so IoU > 0.5 is hard to reach). That is a HYPOTHESIS:
  I did not read p2last PQ, by the pre-registered rule, and the two variants also differ in the
  init (p2first transfers 509/639 pretrained tensors, p2last 555/639).
- Costs: p2first stage 2 ~298 s/epoch vs ~230 for the baseline and ~12-13 GiB of 16 on a T4.

## Decision (per the pre-registered tree)
Structural pass + PQ pass -> 5-fold OOF of `p2first` (folds 1-4 remain; fold 0 done), judged by
pooled paired bootstrap against the Phase-A baseline, with a second baseline seed on each fold to
price noise. p2last is closed. No head/neck tuning, no third variant.
