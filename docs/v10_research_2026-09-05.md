## What the primary sources support

### 1. Mask quality is not detector class confidence

Mask Scoring R-CNN learns a separate mask-IoU predictor because classification
confidence does not reliably track mask overlap. Its evidence is COCO AP with
a trained head, not proof that our frozen refiner's mean output predicts IoU. [Huang et al., CVPR 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.00241)

The official Mask2Former `instance_inference` multiplies class confidence by
mean sigmoid output over its predicted foreground. This provides a concrete
inference heuristic to inspect, not a drop-in calibration guarantee for our
GT-centered crop refiner. [Official implementation, `instance_inference`](https://github.com/facebookresearch/Mask2Former/blob/main/mask2former/maskformer_model.py#L314-L345)

Our proposed adaptation: record foreground mean, probability support inside
the original proposal, and retained area separately. Foreground-only averaging
can reward a tiny confident fragment; original-proposal support can penalize
a valid thin filament inside an over-wide proposal. Neither is automatically
an object-quality measure. Do not average the whole crop, which would confound
quality with the proportion of background.

Modern classifiers can be miscalibrated; that finding motivates caution, not
a claim that we have measured calibration here. Call all these quantities
**proxy scores**, never probabilities of a correct filament. Do not interpret
equal V6/V8 scores as equal reliability or fit a calibrator on tuning/audit data. [Guo et al., ICML 2017](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html)

### 2. Flip agreement is useful evidence, not independent confirmation

Wang et al. use inverse-aligned test-time augmentations to characterize
segmentation variability, with MRI experiments. This supports inspecting
disagreement, but does not validate our four deterministic flips as calibrated
uncertainty or establish a solar-filament acceptance threshold. [Wang et al., Neurocomputing 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.07356)

Detectron2's official TTA first merges detections, applies the same selected
instances across transformed inputs, then inverse-flips and averages their
masks. Instance correspondence precedes averaging. [Official TTA implementation, `_inference_one_image` and `_reduce_pred_masks`](https://github.com/facebookresearch/detectron2/blob/main/detectron2/modeling/test_time_augmentation.py)

For V10, retain fixed-anchor grouping and at most one vote per view. Keep the
existing consensus `sum(view_confidences) / 4`, including zeros for absent
views. Record distinct-view count and mask agreement with the anchor. Four
flips of one model are correlated: consistently wrong detections can survive.
The prior V8 diagnosis already shows that stronger consistency need not imply
better recall. Do not require four-of-four support for all thin/faint objects.

### 3. Preserve instance ownership when rescuing thin masks

Weighted Boxes Fusion averages bounding-box coordinates using confidence; its
published detection gains do not establish that binary-mask union, box overlap,
or equal-weight V6/V8 mask averaging is safe for neighboring filaments. [Solovyev et al., WBF](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.13302)

The clDice work shows why connectivity deserves attention for tubular
segmentation. Its network-topology objective is not our instance PQ objective;
joining two separately annotated filaments can be harmful here. This is a
reason to avoid uncontrolled geometry changes, not to add a topology loss now. [Shit et al., CVPR 2021](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/html/Shit_clDice_-_A_Novel_Topology-Preserving_Loss_Function_for_Tubular_Structure_CVPR_2021_paper.html)

PQ uses matched-mask IoU and penalties for unmatched instances, rather than
an AP precision–recall ranking curve. Its original baseline resolves overlaps
in confidence order, retaining sufficiently intact residual masks. [Kirillov et al., sections 4 and 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1801.00868)

Consequence for this project: changing scores alone cannot improve PQ when
the accepted masks and ownership remain identical. Evaluate final masks after
thresholding and cleanup. Thin-mask rescue should keep V6's pixels immutable,
deduplicate by mask geometry, and add only intact-enough residual candidates.
That is a conservative project design, not a published guarantee of PQ gain.

### 4. Hard negatives require trustworthy negative labels

OHEM selects high-loss training examples and improves region-based detectors;
it motivates exposing the refiner to realistic difficult crops. It does not
justify treating every unmatched proposal as empty background. [Shrivastava et al., CVPR 2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.03540)

Soft Sampling explicitly studies the incorrect background supervision caused
by missing object annotations. Hard mining can amplify that mistake. [Wu et al., BMVC 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.06986)

Our refiner currently sees GT-centered positives; proposal-centered positives
and verified background crops address an untested distribution gap. This does
not establish an inherent incompatibility with V8, nor prove that an empty
refinement means the detector is wrong. Isolate the current empty-mask fallback
before changing training.

## Small predeclared frozen-weight ablation

Recommendation to freeze **before inspecting V10 outcomes**: five initial
configurations, plus at most one conditional combination. These exact values
are project hypotheses, not literature-optimal settings. Record any changes
to this declaration before execution; do not widen the grid after seeing scores.

Keep weights, canonical targets, proposal floor 0.10, detector size 1024,
four-view detector/crop TTA, original split and all non-overlap/area rules fixed.
For V6 use mask threshold 0.40 and consensus cutoff 0.40 throughout.

Definitions, measured in original-image coordinates before fallback/cleanup:

- `P`: the group's untrimmed original anchor proposal.
- `p`: inverse-aligned, four-view-averaged refiner output, resized exactly as in
  the existing pipeline; this is not a calibrated object probability.
- `qP = mean(p[P])`: refiner support on the original proposal; zero for empty P.
- Diagnostics only: `qF = mean(p[R])` on the constrained refined mask R
  (zero when empty), `retained = |P intersection R| / |P|` (zero for empty P), distinct-view count,
  anchor/member mask IoUs, and whether empty-refinement fallback was triggered.

| ID | Only change relative to control | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C0 | None; reproduce the accepted V6 recipe exactly | Is the experiment comparable? |
| E | Reject when the constrained refined mask is empty instead of restoring P | Is fallback rescuing more errors than true objects? |
| Q | Require `qP >= 0.50`; retain original confidence ordering | Does refiner support improve the precision/recall tradeoff? |
| V | Require at least three distinct detector views; retain existing consensus cutoff | Does weak view support explain avoidable errors? |
| R | C0 plus one conservative V8-only rescue recipe below | Is complementary coverage usable without harming V6? |
| X | Best of E/Q/V plus R, only if both that single change and R improve tuning PQ | Do independently useful changes combine? |

Q can also remove empty-refinement cases; E isolates that narrower mechanism.
Do not combine all E/Q/V gates automatically. Use exact tuning PQ as the primary
selection criterion, with C0 winning ties. If eligible E/Q/V variants tie, prefer
E, then Q, then V. X gets one evaluation, not a new parameter search.

R recipe: use V8 consensus cutoff **0.35**, at least **three views**, `qP >= 0.50`,
and the existing V8 mask threshold **0.45**. Reject empty refined rescue masks.
Do not compare or blend V6/V8 numerical scores. These stricter-than-V8-optimum
gates deliberately test precision-first additions, not replacement recall.

Process V8 candidates in deterministic V8-confidence order after V6 is finalized:

1. Compare each full, untrimmed refined candidate with accepted V6 masks and
   earlier accepted V8 rescues. Reject if mask IoU is at least 0.50 **or**
   intersection divided by the smaller mask's area is at least 0.80. The second
   check catches contained fragments that have low IoU.
2. Subtract already-owned pixels from the candidate only. Reject if less than
   90% of its pre-subtraction area remains or residual area is below the
   existing 20-pixel minimum. Never erase or recompute accepted V6 masks.
3. Keep a residual as one instance under existing rules. No union, automatic
   component splitting, extra morphology, or bounding-box-only matching.

These conservative overlap gates can discard genuine neighboring filaments;
report that cost. They protect mask ownership, **not** the resulting score:
additional false positives can still lower PQ.

## Training follow-up only if frozen-weight gains stall

Predeclare a separate training experiment: unchanged positive-only fine-tuning
control, proposal-aware positives only, then the same positives plus verified
negatives. Keep starting checkpoint, optimizer and update budget matched.

- Use original training filenames only. Generate imperfect matched-proposal
  crops alongside GT-centered positives, with separate annotator targets.
  Prefer out-of-fold detector proposals for realistic error mining; ordinary
  training-set predictions are allowed inputs but are in-sample and biased.
- For an **all-zero crop target**, inspect the entire crop, not just proposal
  IoU. A low-IoU mask can still sit over a real filament. Exclude any crop with
  meaningful foreground according to **any** available annotator; combining
  their foreground for negative exclusion is not permission to union training
  instance targets. Absence from every annotation remains insufficient proof
  of background: visually verify candidates and ignore ambiguous/faint cases.
- Do not mine the highest-loss unlabeled crops indiscriminately. As an initial
  project safeguard, cap verified negatives at 25% of sampled crops, retain
  faint positives and avoid filling batches with duplicate neighboring negatives.
  This ratio is a proposed guard, not OHEM's recommended ratio.
- Verify that the loss has finite values and useful background-suppressing
  gradients for all-zero targets; do not assume a Dice-only term handles them.
  Compare full-image canonical PQ, not only crop Dice. Keep an old-refiner
  inference control and evaluate the fallback policy consistently.
- Any future learned scorer belongs on training-only, preferably out-of-fold
  examples, with an independently designed confirmation split—not these held-out
  files. Starting from V8's own refiner is a different ablation, not an assumed fix.
